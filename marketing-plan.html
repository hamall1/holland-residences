Target Audience

The buyer profile for $1.69M+ apartments is narrow. Focus budget on these segments:

Primary: Owner-Occupier Downsizers

  • Age 50-70, couples or singles
  • Currently own a large house in Brisbane's western suburbs
  • Looking for low-maintenance, premium living without leaving the area
  • Interests: property, home design, luxury lifestyle, travel

Secondary: Professional Couples / Young Families

  • Age 30-45, dual income
  • Upgrading from a unit or first home
  • Value proximity to UQ, hospitals, or CBD

Tertiary: Investors

  • Age 35-60, looking for quality assets in Brisbane growth corridors
  • Interested in off-the-plan or new-build opportunities
  • May be interstate (Sydney/Melbourne buyers moving equity to Brisbane)

04

Meta (Facebook + Instagram) Strategy

Facebook Instagram

Meta ads are for creating demand, not capturing it. People aren't searching for apartments - you're putting Holland Residences in front of the right people and making them want to learn more.

Campaign Structure (3-Stage Funnel)

Stage 1: Awareness

Objective: Reach / Video Views

Goal: Get Holland Residences in front of as many qualified eyeballs as possible.

  • Use the best exterior dusk photo or a short video walkthrough
  • Short, punchy copy: "A new benchmark for boutique living in Toowong. Only 9 residences."
  • Target broad demographics in your audience segments
  • Budget: ~30% of Meta spend

Stage 2: Consideration

Objective: Traffic (send to website)

Goal: Drive qualified visitors to explore the residences page.

  • Carousel ad showing: kitchen, living, rooftop, exterior
  • Highlight specific features: "Miele appliances. Travertine stone. Private car lift."
  • Target people who engaged with Stage 1 ads (custom audience)
  • Budget: ~40% of Meta spend

Stage 3: Conversion

Objective: Leads (form submissions)

Goal: Get form submissions from warm prospects.

  • Direct CTA: "Register your interest - only 9 residences available"
  • Social proof: mention "70+ dwellings delivered across Brisbane"
  • Retarget website visitors + Stage 2 engagers
  • Budget: ~30% of Meta spend

Audience Targeting

Audience Targeting
Downsizers Age 50-70, Brisbane, interests: "real estate", "property", "home design", "luxury travel". Household income: top 25%
Professionals Age 30-45, Brisbane, interests: "property investment", "new homes", "architecture". Job titles: manager, director, professional.
Site Retargeting Custom audience of anyone who visited ehmbuild.com.au in the last 30 days (Meta Pixel is already installed)
Lookalike Once you have 50+ enquiries, create a 1% lookalike audience from form submissions

Ad Creative Tips

  • Best performing format: Carousel (multiple images) and single image with strong CTA
  • Use real photos only - the gallery images from the site are perfect. Avoid renders or stock images.
  • Lead with the exterior dusk shot - it's the strongest image and stops the scroll
  • Keep copy short: 2-3 lines max for the primary text on feed ads
  • Always include price: "From $1.69M" qualifies leads upfront so you don't waste budget on people who can't afford it

Example Ad Copy

Primary text: "Nine boutique residences in Toowong. 3 bed, 2 bath, 2 car from $1.69M. Premium finishes throughout - travertine stone, Miele appliances, and a private car lift. Only a few remaining."

UTM Tracking Setup

Critical

Every ad link should include UTM parameters so we can track which ads are generating enquiries in Google Analytics. Use this format for your landing page URLs:

Google Ads

https://ehmbuild.com.au/?utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=holland_search&utm_content=brand_keywords

Facebook / Instagram

https://ehmbuild.com.au/?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=holland_awareness&utm_content=dusk_exterior

Instagram Story

https://ehmbuild.com.au/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=story&utm_campaign=holland_launch&utm_content=carousel_kitchen

How UTMs work: The website already captures these parameters automatically when someone submits the contact form. So when a lead comes in, you can see exactly which ad brought them to the site.

  • Expected leads: 15 - 40 enquiries per month at this budget

How to Actually Run the Ads

Here's the step-by-step process for getting campaigns live on each platform.

Google Ads - Getting Started

  1. Go to ads.google.com and create an account (use the business Gmail)
  2. Click Tools > Linked Accounts > Google Analytics (GA4) and link your GA4 property (G-F8JWGZXZE9)
  3. Go to Goals > Conversions, click "Import", select GA4, and import the generate_lead event as your primary conversion
  4. Click + New Campaign, select "Leads" as the goal
  5. Choose "Search" as campaign type
  6. Set your daily budget (start with $30/day)
  7. Set location to "Brisbane, Queensland" (radius targeting 50km)
  8. Add your keywords from the keyword list in Section 03
  9. Write 3 headlines and 2 descriptions (use the examples in Section 03)
  10. Set the destination URL to: ehmbuild.com.au/?utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=holland_search
  11. Add your negative keywords list
  12. Review, publish, and let it run for 48 hours before making changes

Meta Business - Getting Started

  1. Go to business.facebook.com and create a Business Suite account (or use existing)
  2. Go to Events Manager and verify the Meta Pixel (ID: 2341426659722236) is receiving PageView events from ehmbuild.com.au
  3. In Events Manager, click Custom Conversions and create one for the "Lead" event
  4. Go to Ads Manager > + Create
  5. For your first campaign, select "Traffic" as the objective (Stage 2 - Consideration)
  6. Name it clearly, e.g. "Holland Residences - Consideration - Apr 2026"
  7. At the Ad Set level, set your audience: Location (Brisbane metro), Age, Interests
  8. Set daily budget ($20/day to start for this campaign)
  9. Set placements to Automatic (Meta will optimise across Feed, Stories, Reels)
  10. At the Ad level, upload your best gallery photos as a Carousel
  11. Write the primary text, headline, and description (use examples in Section 04)
  12. Set the destination URL with UTM params
  13. Publish and let it run for 3-4 days before judging performance

Important: Don't touch ads for the first 48-72 hours after launch. Both platforms need time to exit the "learning phase" where they figure out who to show your ads to. Making changes too early resets this process.

Success Metrics - What Good Looks Like

These are the numbers you should be tracking and the benchmarks to aim for.

Primary KPIs (The Ones That Matter)

Metric Target What It Means
Cost Per Lead (CPL) Under $80 How much you're paying for each form submission. This is the most important number. For a $1.69M product, anything under $80 per lead is excellent.
Form Submissions 15-40/month Total enquiries coming through the website. Track weekly.
Conversion Rate 2-5% Percentage of website visitors who submit the form. Below 2% means the ads are bringing the wrong people. Above 5% is exceptional.
Open Home Attendance 5-10 per event Number of qualified buyers physically showing up. This is where deals close.

Secondary KPIs (Health Indicators)

Metric Google Ads Target Meta Target
Click-Through Rate (CTR) Above 3% Above 1%
Cost Per Click (CPC) $3 - $8 $0.50 - $2.00
Bounce Rate Below 60% Below 70%
Avg. Session Duration Above 1:30 Above 1:00
Impressions 5,000+/week 20,000+/week

Red Flags - When to Take Action

  • CTR below 1% on Google: Your ad copy isn't compelling enough, or keywords are too broad. Rewrite headlines.
  • CPC above $12: Keywords are too competitive. Try more specific long-tail keywords like "3 bed apartment Toowong" instead of "apartments Brisbane".
  • High traffic but no leads: The website is getting clicks but no form submissions. Check the landing page experience - is the form easy to find? Is it mobile-friendly?
  • CPL above $150: You're spending too much per lead. Pause the worst-performing ad sets and reallocate budget to what's working.
  • Lots of impressions but few clicks on Meta: Your creative is getting seen but not clicked. Change the image or video - try a different angle.

09

Review and Monitoring Schedule

Don't check stats every hour - it'll drive you crazy and the data isn't meaningful in small samples. Follow this cadence:

Daily (5 minutes)

  • Check total spend is on track (not overspending daily budget)
  • Check for any disapproved ads or policy issues
  • Read any new form submissions that came in
  • Reply to leads within 2 hours during business hours

Weekly (30 minutes - every Monday)

  • Pull Google Ads and Meta dashboards side by side
  • Record in a spreadsheet: spend, clicks, leads, CPL for each platform
  • Check which keywords/audiences are performing best
  • Pause any ad sets with CPL above $150 or CTR below 0.5%
  • Increase budget by 20% on anything with CPL below $50
  • Check Google Analytics: which pages are people viewing? How long are they staying?

Fortnightly (1 hour - every other Friday)

  • Review all leads received - how many converted to inspections?
  • Refresh ad creative if performance is stagnating (ads fatigue after 2-3 weeks)
  • Test new headlines or images (A/B test one variable at a time)
  • Review search terms report in Google Ads - add irrelevant terms as negative keywords
  • Check if open home dates are being promoted in ads (sync with Section 11)

Monthly (Strategy Review)

  • Total leads generated vs target (15-40)
  • Total spend vs budget
  • Lead-to-inspection conversion rate
  • Which platform is delivering better quality leads?
  • Decide whether to scale up, scale down, or reallocate budget between platforms
  • Update ad copy with any new selling points (e.g. "Only 6 remaining")

Pro tip: Create a simple Google Sheet with columns: Week, Platform, Spend, Clicks, Leads, CPL, Notes. Update it every Monday. After 4 weeks you'll see clear trends on what's working and what's not.

Instagram Influencer Strategy (500K Account)

Instagram Organic + Paid

Having access to a 500K follower Instagram account is a massive advantage. This gives you organic reach that would cost thousands in paid ads. Here's how to use it effectively.

Content Plan

Phase 1: Tease (Week 1-2)

Build curiosity before the hard sell.

  • Story 1: "Something exciting is coming to Toowong..." with a cropped detail shot (travertine stone, car lift, rooftop view)
  • Story 2: Behind-the-scenes of the building - show premium finishes being installed
  • Story 3: Sunset/dusk shot of the exterior with "Coming soon" text overlay
  • Use the question sticker "Guess the suburb?" or "What would you want in your dream apartment?"

Phase 2: Reveal (Week 3-4)

Full reveal with direct link to the website.

  • Carousel post: 5-8 best photos (exterior, kitchen, living, rooftop, view). Caption with key details + "Link in bio"
  • Reels: 15-30 second walkthrough set to trending audio. Show the car lift, the rooftop, the kitchen (people love these)
  • Stories with swipe-up: Direct link to ehmbuild.com.au with UTM: ?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=influencer&utm_campaign=sister_account
  • Story poll: "Would you live here? Yes / Take my money" (engagement bait that works)

Phase 3: Open Home Push (Ongoing)

Promote each open home 3-5 days before the event.

  • Story countdown sticker to the open home date
  • Re-share Reels with "This Saturday - come see it for yourself"
  • Post-event recap stories showing the crowd/interest (social proof)

Maximising the 500K Reach

  • Best posting times for Brisbane: Tuesday-Thursday, 7-8am or 7-9pm. Weekends between 10am-12pm.
  • Use location tags: Tag "Toowong", "Brisbane", "Holland Residences" on every post
  • Hashtags: #BrisbaneProperty #ToowongLiving #NewApartments #BrisbaneLuxury #QueenslandRealEstate #InnerCityLiving #BoutiqueApartments
  • Engage with comments: Reply to every comment and DM within 2 hours
  • Boost top posts: If a post gets strong organic engagement, put $20-50 behind it as a boosted post targeting Brisbane 30-65 year olds. This is the best ROI you'll get.

Key advantage: A 500K account posting about Holland Residences gives you instant credibility and social proof. Even if only 2% of followers are in the Brisbane property market, that's 10,000 qualified eyeballs - for free. The paid ads then retarget anyone who engaged with the organic content.

Open Home Plan

Open homes are where deals happen. The marketing drives awareness and leads - the open home closes the sale. Here's how to sync them together.

Suggested Schedule

Timing Day Notes
Launch Open Home Saturday 10am-12pm Big push. First 2 weeks of ads should build towards this date.
Weekly Open Homes Saturdays 10am-11:30am Consistent weekly slot so people know when to come.
Twilight Open Home Wednesday 5pm-6:30pm (monthly) Catches the after-work crowd. The building looks stunning at dusk - use this.
Private Inspections By appointment For serious buyers who've enquired online. Mention this in the form follow-up.

Marketing Sync (2-Week Campaign Cycle)

Week A: Build Up

  • Monday: Update Google Ads with "Open Home This Saturday" ad extensions
  • Tuesday: Instagram Story tease on sister's account (countdown sticker)
  • Wednesday: Meta ad goes live promoting the open home with date/time
  • Thursday: Email all existing leads from the Supabase database inviting them
  • Friday: Final Instagram Story reminder + Reel of the building
  • Saturday: Open Home day. Collect names/emails of attendees on an iPad

Week B: Follow Up + Nurture

  • Monday: Follow up all open home attendees by phone/email within 48 hours
  • Tuesday: Post recap content on Instagram (photos from the open home, attendee quotes if permitted)
  • Wednesday: Review ad performance from the previous week
  • Thursday: Refresh ad creative if needed
  • Friday: Begin building up to next Saturday's open home

On-the-Day Checklist

  • Signage at street level (34 Holland St - Open for Inspection)
  • Fresh flowers, lights on, blinds open, AC set to 23 degrees
  • Printed brochures or floorplans at the entry
  • iPad or sign-in sheet to capture visitor name, email, phone
  • QR code linking to ehmbuild.com.au for people to browse later
  • Suzy (or agent) available for questions throughout
  • Take 2-3 photos/videos for Instagram content (with permission)

Scarcity drives action: As units sell, update both the website and ad copy. "Only 7 remaining" is more compelling than "9 residences available". Every sale should trigger a copy refresh across all channels.
