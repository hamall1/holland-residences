The Vision

A new benchmark
for boutique living

34 Holland Street is not simply a collection of apartments — it is a deliberate response to how people want to live. Nestled in one of Brisbane's most coveted inner-west pockets, this intimate collection of nine residences brings together natural materials, refined craftsmanship, and generous proportions to create homes that feel both elevated and effortless.

Every decision — from the travertine stone and dark timber joinery to the orientation of each floorplan — has been made with intention. The result is a building that feels considered at every turn, with low-maintenance living at its core.
